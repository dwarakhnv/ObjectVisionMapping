What's in it

Cameras

Watch any network (RTSP) or local camera live, with its resolution and details.

 Detect

People, cars, buses, trucks and more outlined on the live video, each with its size, distance and location, plus a live map or 3D view of the environment.

 Environments

Pair video pixels with map points so detections get real-world locations. Camera position, height and angle are estimated from the points. Turn tracking on or off per environment.

 Floor plans

Draw indoor maps to scale (m, ft, …) with walls, rooms, doors and windows, or trace over an image.

 Recordings

Clips saved automatically when people or vehicles of the chosen classes really move (2 m or more). Play them with detections on top, download or delete.

 Remote access API

Access keys per app. Poll, long-poll or stream the environment's objects the moment a new result exists.

How it works

  1. ReadEach camera is read at its own frame rate; the live video never waits for detection.
  2. DetectThe newest frame goes through a YOLO segmentation model, on the Intel GPU (OpenVINO) when there is one.
  3. PlaceWhere an object touches the ground is mapped through the camera's reference points to the map.
  4. MergeAn environment's cameras are combined: one object seen by two cameras is reported once.
  5. ShareApps get the result straight away over the API, filtered to the classes their key allows.

Tracking is off by default: each result lists what is in the latest frame, so nothing is missed while it's being matched up. Turn it on for an environment (Environments page, “Track objects”) to get stable ids, speed, heading and prediction ahead between results. {% if tracking_on %}On now for: {{ tracking_on|join:", " }}.{% endif %}
